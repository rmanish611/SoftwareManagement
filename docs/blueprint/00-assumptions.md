# 00 Assumptions Register

Every default below is a specific, buildable value. Where the owner has already answered, the basis says "Owner instruction". Everything else is my decision under the §9 ladder and is reversible at the cost stated.

| ID | Assumption | Default I chose | Basis | Impact if wrong | Affected REQ IDs | Cost to change |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A-01 | Domain reading | The name "Software Management" is only a folder slug. The system is the corporate website of the owner's own software company: profile, product catalogue, demos, projects, public APIs, lead capture, and sales tracking. It is NOT an IT software-asset/licence-management tool and NOT a marketplace. | PROJECT_BRIEF (intent lock, §6 of the protocol) | Whole blueprint targets the wrong domain | ALL | Total rewrite |
| A-02 | Seller | One company, one owner-operator (a .NET developer in Noida, India), growing to a small team. Single-tenant application: exactly one company's content lives in this database. | Owner instruction | Unnecessary tenant isolation, or a painful retrofit if he ever hosts a second company | ALL | High: TenantId on every table + query filters |
| A-03 | What is sold | Multi-tenant SaaS products (ERP, hospital management, billing, school and college management) sold as a hosted tenant on a plan, plus custom development. No downloadable binaries and no licence keys. | Owner instruction | Licence/download module missing | REQ-CAT-*, REQ-SALE-* | Medium |
| A-04 | Where money is taken | Money is NOT collected on this website in v1. The site captures the lead, the owner sends a quote, and payment happens outside (bank transfer / UPI / a payment link pasted into the quote). The site records the payment, it does not process it. | Owner instruction (payment provider left open) + ADR-06 | A checkout module is missing | REQ-SALE-* | Medium: add a PSP redirect and webhook |
| A-05 | Geography and language | India first, global second. Content in English only in v1; every user-facing string goes through i18n plumbing so Hindi can be added without a rewrite. | Owner is Noida-based, sells to Indian SMBs | Retrofitting i18n across every template | REQ-SITE-*, NFR-I18N-01 | Medium |
| A-06 | Currency | INR is the base currency and the stored currency. Quote and plan amounts carry an explicit currency code; USD display is a later addition, never a silent conversion. | Indian SMB buyers | Wrong totals, wrong tax | REQ-SALE-*, REQ-CAT-* | Medium |
| A-07 | Tax | Indian GST at 18% on software services, stored as a rate per quote line, never hard-coded. GSTIN captured on the organisation. Reverse charge and export-of-service cases are out of scope for v1 and named in the boundary. | Standard Indian SaaS practice | Wrong invoice totals | REQ-SALE-008, REQ-CUST-005 | Low: rate is data |
| A-08 | Timezone rule | Store every instant in UTC (`datetime2`), render in the viewer's timezone, and let the admin set a display timezone (default `Asia/Kolkata`). Scheduled publish/unpublish is entered in the admin's timezone and converted on save. | Umbraco's documented behaviour (S-06) | Content publishes at the wrong hour; SLA timers are wrong | REQ-SITE-006, REQ-LEAD-014 | Low if done from day one, high later |
| A-09 | Peak concurrency | 100 peak concurrent users, 20 writes/sec. | Owner instruction | Over- or under-provisioned; NFR targets meaningless | NFR-PERF-01, NFR-CONC-01 | Low |
| A-10 | Year-1 volume | 2,000 leads, 300 quotes, 60 tenants, 400 content items, 2,000 media files. | Owner instruction (order volumes restated as leads/quotes for this domain) | Index plan and paging wrong | NFR-DATA-01 | Low |
| A-11 | Year-3 volume | 15,000 leads, 2,500 quotes, 300 tenants, 1,500 content items, 10,000 media files. | Owner instruction | Archiving needed sooner | NFR-DATA-02 | Low |
| A-12 | Identity | ASP.NET Core Identity with EF Core stores, JWT access tokens plus rotating refresh tokens. Admin-side only: there is no public customer login in v1, because customers do not self-serve here. | ADR-08; no customer portal in the brief | A customer portal needs a second identity surface | REQ-IAM-* | Medium |
| A-13 | Roles | Four fixed roles: Owner (all), Sales (leads, customers, quotes, tenants), Editor (content, catalogue, projects), Auditor (read-only, including the audit log). | Standard separation of duties | Over-broad access; audit findings | REQ-IAM-004, 06-authz | Low: roles are seeded data |
| A-14 | Email transport | SMTP through MailKit, configured with the owner's own domain and SPF/DKIM. All mail goes through a persisted outbox with retries, never a fire-and-forget `SendAsync`. | S-15 and S-16: contact-form mail silently lands in spam or is never sent | Leads are lost invisibly, the exact failure the research shows is most common | REQ-NOTIF-* | Medium |
| A-15 | Bot protection | Cloudflare Turnstile on every public form, validated server-side against the siteverify endpoint, plus a honeypot field and a per-IP rate limit. | S-11 (Turnstile docs), S-15 | Spam floods the lead inbox and destroys trust in the pipeline | REQ-LEAD-004, REQ-LEAD-005 | Low |
| A-16 | Consent and privacy | India's DPDP Act posture: an explicit, unticked consent checkbox with a purpose string on every form; the consent text, version, timestamp and IP are stored with the submission; a documented erasure path. | Indian law applies to the owner | Legal exposure, no proof of consent | REQ-LEAD-006, REQ-ADM-010 | Medium |
| A-17 | Hosting | One Windows/Linux VM or an App Service in an India region, SQL Server (Standard or Azure SQL S1), behind Cloudflare. Single instance in v1: background jobs assume one scheduler. | Cost, and the owner's own machine is the dev target | Duplicate scheduled sends if scaled out without a distributed lock | NFR-DEP-01, ADR-13 | Medium |
| A-18 | Development database | SQL Server LocalDB `(localdb)\MSSQLLocalDB`, database `SoftwareManagementDb`, accessed through `Microsoft.Data.SqlClient`; `sqlcmd` is not assumed present. | Measured in preflight (08-environment) | Gates cannot verify the database | ALL | Low |
| A-19 | Media storage | Files on local disk under a configured content root with a database row per asset; the storage path goes through an `IFileStorage` abstraction so S3/Azure Blob is a one-class change. Max 10 MB per image, 25 MB per document. | 8 GB dev machine, no cloud budget yet | Migration work when moving to cloud storage | REQ-SITE-009, NFR-DATA-03 | Low |
| A-20 | Demo environments | A live demo is an external URL owned by the owner, with optional shared demo credentials shown on the product page. This site never provisions or hosts the demo; it only links to it and checks that the link is alive. | Brief says "live-demo link" | A provisioning engine would be built that nobody asked for | REQ-CAT-009, REQ-INT-004 | Medium |
| A-21 | Tenant provisioning | Recording a sold tenant is a data entry action plus an optional outbound webhook to the product instance. This site does not create databases or deploy anything. | Scope discipline | A deployment pipeline creeps into a website | REQ-SALE-009, REQ-INT-002 | High |
| A-22 | Public content model | Pages, products, projects and API entries are versioned content with `Draft / Published / Modified` states and scheduled publishing. A published item that is edited keeps serving the published version until the draft is published. | S-05 (Strapi), S-06 (Umbraco) | Editors publish half-finished pages to the public site | REQ-SITE-004, REQ-CAT-011 | High if retrofitted |
| A-23 | SEO | Server-side rendering for public routes (Angular SSR with prerendering), per-page title/description/canonical/OG fields, JSON-LD using `Organization` and `SoftwareApplication`, an auto-generated `sitemap.xml` under the 50,000-URL limit, and a 301 for every changed slug. | S-09, S-10, S-13; RFPs treat SEO as contractual (S-14) | Marketing site that search engines cannot read: the whole point of the site is lost | REQ-SITE-011, REQ-INT-006 | High |
| A-24 | Browser matrix | Last two versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, plus Chrome and Safari on mobile. No Internet Explorer. Mobile-first layout: over half the traffic is assumed to be phones. | Standard 2026 baseline | Layout complaints from real buyers | NFR-BROW-01 | Low |
| A-25 | Accessibility | WCAG 2.2 Level AA, verified by an automated axe check in CI plus a manual keyboard pass per phase. | S-01; Indian and US public-sector RFPs make it contractual (S-14) | Excludes users and fails any government tender | NFR-ACC-01 | Medium |
| A-26 | Retention | Leads and submissions: 3 years after last activity, then anonymised. Audit log: 7 years, append-only. Email delivery log: 12 months. Backups: 30 daily, 12 monthly. | DPDP storage limitation and Indian bookkeeping practice | Unbounded growth; legal exposure | NFR-RET-01, REQ-ADM-011 | Low |
| A-27 | Analytics | First-party page-view and form-funnel counters stored in this database. A third-party tag (for example Google Analytics) is optional, loaded only after cookie consent, and never required for the owner's own reports. | Cookie consent under DPDP; the owner wants numbers he controls | Reports depend on an external tool the owner may not have | REQ-RPT-001, REQ-INT-008 | Low |
| A-28 | Response-time promise | The site promises a first response to any enquiry within one business day (09:00-18:00 IST, Monday to Saturday, excluding configured holidays). This is the SLA the dashboard measures. | Realistic for a one-person shop; RFPs demand a stated response time | Broken public promise; wrong SLA maths | REQ-LEAD-014, NFR-PERF-05 | Low |
| A-29 | Day-one data | The owner starts with an Excel sheet of past enquiries and a folder of project screenshots. A CSV/XLSX importer with a dry-run preview and a per-row error report is in scope for products, projects, leads and organisations. | Every real go-live starts from a spreadsheet | Manual re-typing; the site launches empty | REQ-ADM-006, REQ-ADM-007 | Medium |
| A-30 | Team growth | Up to 5 admin users. No approval workflow for content in v1 beyond draft/publish, but every change is audited and reversible through content versions. | One-person shop today | A missing approval chain if a junior editor is hired | REQ-SITE-005, REQ-ADM-009 | Medium |
